Before You Leave

Read this part twice

What changed since v2, and the permit rules that decide whether Zero Point and North Sikkim even happen for you.

What changed from v2
  • Departure moved to the night of 13 Nov 2026 — absorbs the old 1-day overflow.
  • The extra day you offered went to Darjeeling's 3rd night — toy train, viewpoints and tea estate now spread across two relaxed days.
  • Visa steps removed — yours is already issued for Burimari–Changrabandha, not Banglabandha.
  • Every day now has clock times and named pickup/drop-off points.
Sikkim permit reality check (updated)
  • The old Ministry of Home Affairs pre-clearance requirement for Bangladeshi nationals specifically was lifted back in Nov 2018 — that rule now applies only to Pakistan, China, Myanmar, Nigeria, Hong Kong and Taiwan nationals, not Bangladesh.
  • Bigger change: since 12 Jan 2026, Sikkim stopped issuing RAP/PAP on the spot at Rangpo/Melli entirely. All foreign nationals must apply online in advance via the e-FRRO portal (indianfrro.gov.in) — RAP is usually self-filed, PAP is normally routed through your registered agent.
  • Apply after you've crossed into India but a few days before you reach Rangpo — approvals typically take 24–48 hrs. The SNT Complex stop in Siliguri is a good place to double-check your agent has this in hand, but it's no longer where the permit itself gets sorted.
  • Solo foreigners must travel in a group of 2+ in permit zones — a shared group tour seat satisfies this.
  • Zero Point is now one of the three zones (with Tsomgo Lake and Yumthang Valley) explicitly open to foreign nationals through this online system — still get it confirmed in writing, since access can change.
  • Nathu La, Baba Mandir, Gurudongmar Lake remain closed to all foreign nationals and are not part of this plan.

Documents to carry

Originals + 2–3 photocopies of each — passport & visa, 6–8 photos, hotel confirmations, bus tickets, NID copy, and your printed e-FRRO permit approvals (RAP + PAP).

Money

Small BDT→INR (or a little USD) for the first hours; withdraw the bulk from a Siliguri ATM; keep Rs.100/500 notes for jeeps and small shops.

Packing

Heavy jacket, thermals, gloves, wool cap; sturdy shoes for Tiger Hill; power bank, rain jacket, padlock, medication & altitude tablets.

Section 2

Dhaka → Siliguri, timed

Burimari–Changrabandha crossing, Friday night into Saturday morning.

~8:00–8:30 PM · Fri 13 Nov
Board your AC coach at the Dhaka counter (Gabtoli/Kalyanpur). Confirm the service crosses at Burimari, matching your visa.
~9:00 PM – 5:30 AM
Overnight journey north through the Rangpur route towards Burimari, Lalmonirhat. Try to sleep.
~6:00–7:00 AM · Sat 14 Nov
Arrive Burimari land port. Breakfast at a stall near the terminal before immigration opens.
~7:00–8:00 AM
Bangladesh customs & emigration at Burimari — passport stamped out. Keep your bag accessible for a check.
~8:00–8:15 AM
Short walk across the zero point to the Indian side at Changrabandha, Cooch Behar district.
~8:15–9:00 AM
Indian immigration & customs at Changrabandha — passport and visa stamped in. Your Sikkim RAP/PAP must already be approved on e-FRRO before this point — it's checked electronically further along, not stamped fresh at Rangpo like before.
~9:00–9:30 AM
Onward local transport (shared bus/jeep) towards Siliguri — board near the Changrabandha bus stand.
~9:30 AM–12:00 PM
Road journey Changrabandha → Siliguri (~90–100 km via Mainaguri/Jalpaiguri, ~2.5–3 hrs).
~12:00–12:30 PM
Arrive Siliguri — drop near Tenzing Norgay Bus Terminus / SNT Complex, Hill Cart Road, Pradhan Nagar.
Some operators sell a single through-ticket that carries you all the way to Siliguri — you may not need to arrange the Changrabandha–Siliguri leg yourself. Confirm this when you book.
